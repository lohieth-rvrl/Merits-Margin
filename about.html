About Ledger & Route

Ledger & Route covers the two decisions that shape most people's financial lives: how you manage money, and how you build a career worth managing money from.

We write plainly, avoid jargon, and try to explain not just what to do but why it works. We don't give personalized financial or legal advice -- our goal is to make the underlying ideas clear enough that you can make your own informed decision, or know the right questions to bring to a professional.

What we cover

Three sections: Finance for budgeting, saving, credit and investing; Career & Jobs for resumes, negotiation and the job market; and Money News, where we translate economic headlines into what they actually mean for your day-to-day decisions.

A note on independence

This site is supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdSense and are not personally endorsed by our editorial team. Editorial coverage is not influenced by advertisers.
