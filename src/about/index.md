<section class="simple-page wrap">
  <h1>About Ledger & Route</h1>
  <p class="lede">Ledger & Route covers the two decisions that shape most people's financial lives: how you manage money, and how you build a career worth managing money from.</p>
  <p>We write plainly, avoid jargon, and try to explain not just what to do but why it works. We don't give personalized financial or legal advice -- our goal is to make the underlying ideas clear enough that you can make your own informed decision, or know the right questions to bring to a professional.</p>
  <h2>What we cover</h2>
  <p>Three sections: <strong>Finance</strong> for budgeting, saving, credit and investing; <strong>Career &amp; Jobs</strong> for resumes, negotiation and the job market; and <strong>Money News</strong>, where we translate economic headlines into what they actually mean for your day-to-day decisions.</p>
  <h2>A note on independence</h2>
  <p>This site is supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdSense and are not personally endorsed by our editorial team. Editorial coverage is not influenced by advertisers.</p>
