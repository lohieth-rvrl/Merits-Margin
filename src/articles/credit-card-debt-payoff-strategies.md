## The avalanche method

Pay minimums on everything, then throw every extra dollar at the card with the **highest interest rate** first. Mathematically, this saves the most money over time, because you stop the most expensive debt from compounding first.

## The snowball method

Pay minimums on everything, then throw every extra dollar at the **smallest balance** first, regardless of interest rate. It costs more in total interest than the avalanche method, but it produces a paid-off card faster, which for many people is the difference between staying motivated and giving up.

## Which one actually works better

The honest answer is whichever one you'll stick with. If you're confident interest math alone will keep you motivated, avalanche saves real money. If your past attempts at debt payoff have stalled out, the quick wins from snowball are worth the extra interest cost.

## When consolidation makes sense

A balance-transfer card or personal loan can help if you qualify for a meaningfully lower rate and you have a concrete plan to avoid re-running the balance back up. Consolidation without a spending plan just moves the debt -- it doesn't reduce it.
