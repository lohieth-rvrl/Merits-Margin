## What makes a remote role durable, not just trendy

The most reliable remote roles share two traits: the output is measurable independent of location, and the field has an ongoing skills shortage. Roles that are easy to measure and hard to staff tend to stay remote-friendly even as companies pull other roles back to offices.

## Fields worth a closer look

- **Cloud and DevOps engineering** -- steady demand tied to infrastructure that rarely needs to be in-person
- **Technical/product-adjacent writing** -- documentation and content roles that support software teams
- **Data and analytics** -- especially roles with SQL and dashboarding skills
- **Customer success for B2B software** -- relationship-driven but tool-based, so location rarely matters

## How to actually break in

Certifications matter less than a visible portfolio. A public GitHub project, a written case study, or a small freelance client result demonstrates capability faster than a credential alone -- especially for remote roles, where a hiring manager can't observe you in person.
