## There is no single right answer

"Is now a good time" is really three separate questions: what's happening with prices, what's happening with borrowing costs, and what's happening in your own finances and life plans. Media coverage usually only addresses the first two.

## The market-level factors worth checking

- Local inventory levels -- more listings generally means more negotiating room
- How long homes are sitting on the market in your target area
- Whether local prices are still rising, flat, or falling year-over-year

## The personal factors that matter more than timing the market

How long you plan to stay in the home matters more than short-term price movement -- a home held for 7-10+ years tends to smooth out short-term market swings. Your job stability and the size of your emergency fund after the down payment matter just as much as the interest rate you lock in.
